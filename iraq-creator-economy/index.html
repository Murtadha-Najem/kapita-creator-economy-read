The phone became the land

Turning Influence into IndustryFrom Followers to Founders

In Iraq, influencers are not just advertisers: they are brand founders. The influencer is not a marketing channel: they are the competition.

Interactive ReadApril 2026

Kapita ResearchBaghdad

Reading time8 minutes

Scroll to read

1

Chapter 1: The trust vacuum

What makes Iraq structurally distinctive is that none of the usual friction points apply: there is no credible legacy media competing for audience trust, and no developed retail infrastructure requiring expensive entry.

Global brands

“Iraqi-made” is a premium signal

“Iraqi-made” functions as a premium signal in a market where global brands are viewed with suspicion inherited from decades of sanctions.

In the post-October 2023 environment of international brand boycotts, domestic origin signals carry purchasing power beyond functional value.

SourceKapita Research, Turning Influence into Industry, 2026, pp. 9 and 46

Internet penetration in Iraq

One of the fastest in MENA

Penetration crossed 80% for the first time in 2022 and 2023, driven by mobile broadband rollout, the affordability of Chinese-manufactured smartphones, and COVID-era demand for connectivity.

A mobile-first generation

An alternative economic model

42%of Iraq's population is under 25

The median age is 20.8, youth unemployment is 32.09%, and approximately 700,000 school and college leavers a year compete for scarce public-sector positions and a thin private sector. The private-label brand that follows is proof that the model scales.

SourceTrading Economics / ILO, via the report, p. 15

Dialect

Dialect as a trust signal

When an Iraqi creator speaks dialect, they signal peer membership.

The Communications and Media Commission's own 2023 framework regulations acknowledge this, permitting advertising in “standard Arabic language or the local Iraqi dialect that is understandable”.

SourceKapita Research, p. 15

2

Chapter 2: Unmatched audience density

A creator who masters short-form video can achieve national coverage without a television network, and followers purchase as an extension of the relationship, not merely as a market transaction.

Top platforms by ad reach

TikTok reaches 90%+ of users

Instagram's 21.5M users are disproportionately in the 18 to 34 demographic, and it is the beauty and fashion discovery platform.

WhatsApp

The sale closes on WhatsApp

TikTok is the primary discovery, Instagram is beauty and fashion discovery, and WhatsApp is the backbone of social commerce: order fulfillment, delivery, customer service.

SourceKapita Research, pp. 9 and 14

The four-stage pipeline

Four stages

An influencer accumulates taste authority and domain credibility, then converts it into economic capital through a private-label product that embodies their persona.

Cultural authority

Authority without challenge

Without credible legacy critics, professional reviewers or trusted consumer guides, the beauty creator becomes the equivalent of a beauty editor, and the food creator the equivalent of a restaurant critic.

SourceKapita Research, p. 19

3

Chapter 3: The creator becomes the product

At stage four the creator does not endorse another company's product: the brand name is the creator's name, the packaging carries their face, the origin story behind the brand is their origin story.

The archetype

Blogger to billion-dollar brand

$1.2BHuda Beauty valuation (2017 TSG stake)

Huda Kattan launched a beauty blog in 2010 and a product in 2013 with USD 6,000, the first influencer-led brand at Sephora Dubai. In June 2025 she returned Huda Beauty to full founder ownership.

SourceBusiness of Fashion and others, via the report, p. 22

Iraqi creators

Iraq is the #1 market

Iraq is the #1 market for By Mina Al Sheikhly, ahead of Saudi Arabia and the UAE. In Baghdad, architect Shams Al-Khazraji founded AlMaqsad, a beauty distribution network, and then Voyage Cosmetics, priced at IQD 12,000 to 35,000.

SourceForbes Middle East and the brands' pages, via the report, pp. 23 and 24

When the founder is the character

His face on the packaging

The Abu Jannah brand has no mascot and no jingle, and his personal social media accounts are the primary marketing channel. Every prize delivery is filmed and posted: the delivery theatre is not logistics, it is content production, building a geographic social proof chain across governorates, from Baghdad to Salah al-Din.

SourceKapita Research, p. 34

Beyond consumer goods

That audience belongs to Talib

Institutional brand spend in Iraq is, in part, involuntary creator incubation.

First Iraqi Bank's Pocket Fight campaign grew jazz artist Mahmood Talib's following from 7,000 to 500,000 in under two months, and the TCL Bling campaign achieved 30 million+ organic Instagram views. The same pipeline extends to real estate.

SourceAds of the World, via the report, p. 38

4

Chapter 4: Prize programme economics

How is Abu Jannah's monthly car-giveaway programme financially sustainable?

Three go-to-market models

Why the name-only model fails

Abu Jannah and Skonono sit in the P&L partnership and full-ownership columns: high founder skin-in-the-game. The short-lived Celebrity Chips wave clusters in the fixed-fee column: no founder risk, no operational accountability.

The price ladder, per carton

From factory to shelf

Manufacturing cost is 30% or less of retail. The consumer mass pack is 250 IQD, the dominant price point, and the family pack 500 IQD.

Distribution

Distribution, not production

100,000+points of sale, replenished

Shops reorder 7 times a month, approximately 21 cartons per shop, a cadence consistent with replenishment demand rather than novelty trial.

SourceJamila supplier panel data, April 2026, via the report, p. 31

Headline vs. conservative economics

0.7% of gross profit

A stress case with higher operating costs still leaves monthly net profit of about $0.93M, sufficient to fund ten times the current prize cadence.

5

Chapter 5: Real constraints

Ignoring them produces a distorted picture of how Iraqi creator brands actually function.

The regulatory environment is accelerating

Five steps in three years

The compliance burden pushes influencer payments toward cash and proxy accounts, reducing financial transparency for creator-brands seeking formal investment or international partnerships.

Platform ban risk

The ban risk is real

Creator brands built primarily on TikTok discovery face platform concentration risk that Instagram-first or WhatsApp-native brands do not.

Morality regulations

Even comedy videos

1,500+morality-linked cyber cases since 2023, with over 200 arrests

Article 403 of the 1969 Iraqi Penal Code is the primary prosecution instrument. With the Ballegh platform for anonymous citizen reporting, 500+ violations and 75 arrests were recorded in January to March 2025 alone.

SourceShafaq News, 2025, via the report, p. 42

Counterfeits

Counterfeit leakage

In food, the Abu Jannah prize-chip format has spawned imitators whose prize delivery infrastructure is unverified, risking reputational contamination of the original.

In short

The most efficient route

In Iraq, the most efficient route to a national consumer brand in 2026 runs not through retail distribution or above-the-line advertising, but through a creator's parasocial relationship with their audience.

The pipeline is fast, low-friction, and increasingly contested. For global brands entering Iraq, the influencer is not a distribution partner: they are a competitor.
